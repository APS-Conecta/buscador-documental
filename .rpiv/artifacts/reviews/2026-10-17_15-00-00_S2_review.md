## Accepted / deferred (with reason)
- Shared `/work` between supervisor and parser (A2): exploitable only after a prior compromise of a parser dependency plus a `setsid` escape. Mitigated by `O_NOFOLLOW`, answer validation and purge-at-start; per-job directories with distinct uids are an S7 hardening item.
- `image_heavy` re-reads good scanner layers and OCR runs the page twice (confidence pass): performance, S5.
- `MAX_BYTES`/`MAX_PAGES` duplicated between `main.py` and `extract.py`; zombie-leak and `.err` flood not reproduced end to end (reasoned).
- Retryable `ocr_failed` on deterministic failures is bounded by the S1 attempts cap (5).

## Refuted or corrected
- "Inherited `/Resources` bypasses the pixel check": false (pikepdf resolves inheritance; reproduced `Unsafe`).
- "1280x720 logo triggers `image_heavy`": false, 921 600 px is under the 1 000 000 threshold (a 1920x1080 photo would).
- Reviewer line numbers were often off; the numbers above are the verifier's.
